    What this means for step 2

    1. Build the TeeItUp adapter first. TeeItUp is GolfNow's booking engine. Jackson Park and every 18-hole Cook County Forest Preserve course use it, and most GolfNow-listed courses run on it or show the same inventory.
    2. EZLinks is a clear second. It covers Harborside, The Glen Club, Wilmette, Arrowhead, Ruffled Feathers, Tamarack, Willow Crest, Bridges of Poplar Creek and Foss Park.
    3. Club Prophet (CPS) is third. It covers all three Lake County Forest Preserve courses, Brighton Dale Links, Glencoe, Mistwood and Odyssey.
    4. Link out for the rest. Chronogolf, foreUP, Teesnap and the one-off systems each cover only a handful of courses. They get a "Check times" link to begin with.

    Every link goes to the booking page on the course's own website where one was found. "Course website" means the link is the course's tee-time page, but the system behind it wasn't identified yet. Most of these sites say "Powered by GolfNow", so they are likely TeeItUp. Eight courses only showed up on GolfNow and have no booking link yet.
